Hierarchy 03 · Designer's Choice

Retro Arcade

Silkscreen, a pixel font, for the big headings, paired with Nunito, a soft rounded sans-serif, for reading. Built on a Perfect Fifth (1.5) modular scale for dramatic jumps in size.

Insert Coin

Before home consoles, games lived in loud, glowing rooms full of cabinets. You walked in with a pocket of quarters and walked out when they ran out — or when someone beat your high score.

Three lives. One quarter. No saves.

The arcade was a social place. People gathered behind the best players just to watch. Learn more about the golden age of arcade games.

High Score Culture

Typing three letters next to your score was a small way to become famous. Your initials stayed on the screen for every player who came after you, at least until the machine was unplugged.

Why a Pixel Font

Silkscreen is built on a square grid, just like the text on old game screens, so it sets the mood instantly. But pixel fonts are tiring to read in long passages. Nunito's rounded letters echo the playful feel while staying comfortable for paragraphs.

Press Start

Many cities still have barcades where you can play the classics. Find a retro arcade near you and go chase a high score.
