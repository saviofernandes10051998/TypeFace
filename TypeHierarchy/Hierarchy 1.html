Hierarchy 01 · Two Typefaces

Late Night Jazz Club

Bodoni Moda, a high-contrast display serif, paired with Work Sans, a clean and friendly sans-serif. Built on a Perfect Fourth (1.333) modular scale.

The Music Starts After Midnight

Some clubs close their doors when the crowd thins out. The best ones are just warming up. Down a narrow staircase, behind a heavy red door, the stage lights dim and a trumpet player tests a single long note.

The room goes quiet before the first song.

The real show happens in the second set, when the band stops playing the songs on the menu and starts listening to each other. You can read more about the history of the after-hours jam session and how it shaped modern jazz.

Tonight's Lineup

The house trio opens at 11 p.m. with standards. At 1 a.m. the doors open to any musician who brought an instrument. Nobody knows what will happen next, and that is the whole point.

Why Bodoni and Work Sans

Bodoni Moda has thick and thin strokes that feel like a vintage concert poster. It brings the glamour. Work Sans is calm and easy to read at small sizes, so it carries the longer paragraphs without fighting the headlines. One typeface shows off; the other does the explaining.

Plan Your Visit

Doors open at 10 p.m. There is a two-drink minimum and no cover charge on weeknights. See the full monthly calendar or reserve a table near the stage.
