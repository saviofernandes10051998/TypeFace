Hierarchy 02 · One Typeface

Chicago Transit Guide

Barlow alone, from Light 300 to ExtraBold 800. Every level is made with weight, size, case and color — no second typeface. Built on a Major Third (1.25) modular scale.

Riding the L

Chicago's elevated trains run on eight color-coded lines that meet in the Loop. If you know the color and the direction, you can get almost anywhere in the city.

Tip: always check which direction the train is heading before you board.

Fares are paid with a Ventra card or a phone tap at the turnstile. A printable system map is a good thing to keep in your bag on your first week.

The Lines at a Glance

  • Red Line — north and south, runs 24 hours
  • Blue Line — O'Hare to Forest Park, runs 24 hours
  • Brown Line — Kimball to the Loop
  • Pink Line — 54th/Cermak to the Loop

Signage Lesson

Barlow was inspired by the signs on California highways and transit systems. Its slightly rounded, condensed shapes stay readable from far away, which is why it fits a wayfinding guide. Using one family keeps the page feeling like an official system.

Getting to Campus

From the Loop, take the Red, Brown or Purple Line north to Fullerton for DePaul's Lincoln Park campus. Plan a trip with live arrival times.
